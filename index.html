How Coverage Compass™ Works

A simple, guided journey to understanding your coverage.

Step 1: Answer Simple Questions

Tell us about your situation — your family, home, vehicles, and priorities. No jargon, no pressure.

Our system learns what matters most to you and tailors the experience accordingly.

📄

Step 2: Get Your Checklist

Receive a personalized coverage checklist showing areas you may want to review with a professional.

This is educational — not a policy recommendation. You're in control.

✅

Step 3: Ask the AI

Use our AI educational companion to understand insurance concepts in plain English.

It uses DFIS-approved content and clearly discloses when you should talk to a human.

🤖

Step 4: Connect with an Agent

When you're ready, book an appointment, request a callback, or submit your information.

Your agent receives your full context — no need to start from scratch.
